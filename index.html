The gap

Nobody owns it, because it sits between two teams and belongs to neither

Creative teams cannot originate this work. Not for want of skill. Writing an evaluation guide means knowing what model FLOPs utilization is, why goodput matters more than peak FLOPS, what an hour of ETTR is worth across a 1,024 GPU run, and what actually happens when a node fails four days into training.

Engineering can explain all of it and will not prioritize writing it, because it is not the product. So the layer stays empty while the buyer is left to assemble the case from documentation, a press release and a benchmark PDF on a CDN.

Meanwhile the decision is being made. Usually by someone who has to walk into a room and justify a commitment in the tens of millions of dollars.
